NATIONALITY QUEST - OFFLINE SMARTBOARD SITE

How to open
1. Double-click index.html.
2. Use a modern browser such as Chrome or Edge.
3. Press F or the Full screen button for smartboard use.

Classroom flow
- Part 1: Who is this? (character names)
- Part 2: Where is ... from? (country names, then complete sentences)
- Part 3: Is he/she/it ...? (short answers, then extended answers)

Keyboard controls
- 1-4: choose an answer
- N or Right Arrow: next question
- L: read the question aloud
- F: full screen

The site is fully static. It does not require an internet connection, a server,
Tailwind, npm, or any installation. Keep the assets folder next to index.html.
